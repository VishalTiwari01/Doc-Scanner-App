# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: June 7, 2026**

DocMaster AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DocMaster AI mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### A. Personal Data
While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information includes:
* **Account Credentials**: Full Name, Email Address, and encrypted passwords. Used solely for account setup, authentication, security, and OTP (One-Time Password) transmission.

### B. User Content & Uploaded Assets
To perform the core features of the Service (image compression, JPG to PDF conversion, OCR text extraction), the application uploads assets to our servers:
* **Scanned Images & Documents**: Photos taken with your camera or chosen from your library are transmitted securely to our backend server and uploaded to secure hosting (Cloudinary) to compile PDFs, compress file sizes, or extract text.
* **OCR Output**: Extracted texts and associated structured fields (like PAN, Aadhaar, and Passport numbers) are stored securely in our database to compile your user history logs.
* **Activity Logs**: We record sizes, timestamps, and metadata (original file sizes vs. compressed file sizes) to display stats on your Home dashboard.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Required

To operate the Service successfully, the application requests access to the following native device capabilities:
* **Camera Permission**: Required to capture documents, certificates, and photos directly inside the app for instant OCR or PDF generation.
* **Storage / Photos Permission**: Required to select existing images from your gallery and to download or save optimized PDF and image outputs directly to your device storage.

---

## 3. Data Deletion and Control (Your Rights)

We believe in full user ownership of personal data.
* **Record Deletion**: You can delete any document optimization or OCR record from your activity logs at any time using the red "Delete" action button on the History screen.
* **Database Purge**: When a record is deleted, it is permanently purged from our database collections, and the files are unlinked.
* **Account Removal**: To request complete deletion of your account credentials, contact our support team.

---

## 4. Service Providers (Third-Party Subprocessors)

We employ third-party companies and services to facilitate our Service, process operations on our behalf, or perform Service-related tasks:
* **Cloudinary**: Used as secure cloud storage to host compressed documents and previews temporarily.
* **MongoDB Atlas**: Used to host database clusters for account profiles and metadata history.
* **Nodemailer / SMTP (Gmail)**: Used to deliver verification OTPs to your registered email address for password resets.

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal documents or account data to third-party advertising companies.

---

## 5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We encrypt password strings using bcrypt hashing before database entry and utilize secure transport protocols (HTTPS) to safeguard your uploads.

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.
