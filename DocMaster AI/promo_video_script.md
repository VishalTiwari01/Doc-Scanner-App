**Brand Colors**: Indigo (`#4361EE`), Blue (`#4895EF`), Charcoal Dark, Clean White  
**Vibe**: Fast-paced, modern, premium, intelligent

### Voiceover & Production Notes:
* **VO Artist Tone**: Friendly, energetic, clear, and confident.
* **Pacing**: High energy but articulate. The script contains roughly 80 words, which is ideal for a 35-40 second voiceover, leaving room for transitions and sound effects.
* **Accessibility**: Keep text overlays high-contrast (using the brand's primary Indigo `#4361EE`) so users browsing on mute get 100% of the value message.
